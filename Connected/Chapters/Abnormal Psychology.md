**Zoey**

Zoey looked down at her phone, her step was extra perky and bubbly today. The photo of her cousin finally admitting what she always knew had kept her at high spirits all day. Those two didn't need to hide their love behind closed doors anymore.

She wondered how long it would be until Astrid discovered Cale’s job as a super spy. He once described what he did to her and it seemed confusing and fake, people couldn’t *possibly* need that much coordination just to write code. So Zoey knew it was a cover up.

She did a little twirl of giddyment. Her long perfectly brushed black hair, splashed with several sections of pink, spun around her, matched perfectly with a twirl of her flowing white sundress.

She delighted in the complexities of her cousin's love life. His life was so much more interesting than hers.

She already had a job lined up as an assistant costume designer for a movie production company. She had perfect grades that seemed to come with no effort. And she had a distinctive charm that had a tendency to get whatever she wanted, and in very, very, rare cases, whoever she wanted. Not that she ever really wanted things.

Well, except a friend.

She had observed that people bond over difficulties, not successes. It was the core of romance, in fact. Her life had no such difficulties. So while, much like her cousin, she had a vast network of social connections, and people she would hang out with, it was all very… parasocial. She was surely the most well loved, yet unapproachable, girl who had ever existed.

So she lived in the fantasies of other people's love lives. She knew it would be only a year before those two got married. He was probably going to whisk her off to some romantic getaway way too soon. Her heart fawned over the image that played out in her head. A love sick Astrid, carried romantically onto a cruise ship destined for some exotic location. She had to repress a shiver of excitement.

Of course her distinct lack of focus meant when she rounded a corner leading up to her next class, she walked smack dab into someone. They tumbled to the ground, a cliche amount of books and papers spilling around them.

“Oh my goodness! I'm so sorry!” Zoey's mind snapped to the present, reaching down to grab books. A delicate female hand reached out for the same book and accidentally touched hers. Oh… oh! Just like the movies!

She looked up at her new potential love interest, eyes widening and ready to blink several times to sell the love struck look.

The other girl just pushed her hand aside and grabbed the book, mumbling an apology. She didn't even meet eyes. Does she not know how this scene is supposed to go? Maybe she's not into girls. Oh well.

“Do you need help?” Zoey stood up, offering a hand to help the book laden girl up. She had striking short red hair with a touch of wave to it that curled messing into her face. Patches of freckles splashed around brilliant green eyes.

Zoey sized her up, a green open fronted Hoodie wrapped over a black t-shirt with a pink haired J Pop idol, and loose, but not quite baggy jeans. It wasn’t a bad look, with that figure she would tighten the jeans up, a few splotches of green in the hair would tie the whole red-and-green theme together well. She shook the thought off, realizing she was getting distracted as usual.

The girl didn't pay much attention to her, she took the offered hand but scurried off. Zoey found herself watching the girl run off… intrigued. She couldn't think of the last time she had been so blatantly ignored. The thought escaped as she realized the time, she couldn't keep being late due to random distractions of the day.

**Naina**

Several hours later Naina found herself sitting in the dorm lounge, re-reading the same passage in an Abnormal Psychology textbook. Her thoughts were loud today, and she hadn't been quite able to wrangle her focus into schoolwork.

Her therapist had suggested that she try reading out where there was ambient noise. Music often helped her but on days like this it seemed she needed something else. Her grades needed to improve though, she couldn't afford another year beyond next year and she would not graduate Senior year at this rate.

The reality was that she needed structure though, something to orient herself around. She didn’t know what she wanted to do with her degree, only that psych classes were the only ones she didn’t utterly fail. So the future was a confusing mess of unknowns resulting in compounding anxiety.

“You!” She heard an enthusiastic voice, she didn't look up. No one would be addressing her.

“What are the chances we are in the same dorm? I've never seen you before, are you a freshman? What are you studying?” Naina felt a presence approach her, she blinked and looked up. It was that girl from before, she was leaning over to read the textbook, several long strands of her hair fell to Naina's hands.

“About one in ten, I'm a Sophomore, and uh… what was the third one? One at a time please.” Naina ducked her head down to get a look at the girl's face.

“Abnormal Psychology, that's cool.” She sat down on the couch. Naina furrowed her brow, this girl had the exact kind of energy she typically avoided. Well… her parents kept demanding she make a friend.

“They say most Psych Majors study it to understand their own… uniqueness. I feed that stereotype.” Naina smiled.

“Huh. Wonder what they say about fashion majors.” The girl asked, taking the effort to give an exaggerated thoughtful look to the ceiling.

“Control and obsession of aesthetics is often masking behavior for insecurities around one's self perception of worth.” Naina observed. The girl's animated body froze, her face flickered between a frown and a fairly fake smile. Naina realized her mistake.

“But like, also, clothes are pretty.” She quickly added, feeling a touch of blush take over her.

The girl gave a short nervous laugh, then her body relaxed, “Yes, clothes are pretty.” Was that a smile? Naina was confused, there was clearly some piece of social nuance she was missing. She had just insulted this random stranger, hadn't she?

“Zoey.” The girl extended a hand, maintaining the smile. Naina eyed the hand warily.

“I don't understand, I'm sorry.”

“Do I need to spell it out for you?” Zoey teased. Naina tilted back the book which clearly read “Abnormal Psychology” on the front cover, a flat look on her face.

Zoey's hand went to her mouth, and she giggled. People actually giggled?

“Friends. I want to be friends.” She said

Well, *now* it seemed obvious.

“Why?” Naina asked without thinking, quickly adding “Wait, no you don't need to answer that. Sorry, sometimes my words are faster than my brain.”

Zoey contemplated, “I don't know why. Want to go watch a movie?”

Naina gawked, “Wait, you can just… ask people to do things?”

“Why not?” The girl literally was bouncing, with enthusiasm, waiting for a response.

Naina chewed on this. Of course you couldn't… there were social contracts, a delicate balance of expectations that must be met before you just… ask people for things. Right? Or was that her? It was so hard to separate her from…. Her.

“I mean, I don't have a counterpoint, but I'm not sure that means you're right.” Naina stared at her textbook. There was no way she would make more progress. Her mind was on this bubbly girl and her supernatural ability to just ask for what she wanted. Something about watching tacky movies with her clicked into a light fixation.

Zoey toned down the bouncing and simply sat there patiently, her head bobbing from side to side to a tune no one else could hear.

“OK. What are we watching?” Naina gathered her books and papers up and stood.

“Super cheesy romance movies.” Zoey said with delight, hopping off the couch.

“You are very decisive.”

“Yes.” Zoey beamed, then looked at Naina’s books, “I can get you a backpack. Or a messenger bag.”

“I don't like straps on my shoulders.” Naina asserted.

The two walked off down the hallway, Naina walking backwards facing Zoey, discussing the practicality of various wheel based carrying devices.
