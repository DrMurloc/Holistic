Finding value in others that no one else can see. But... He's holding onto a *lot*. I suspect there are pieces of Astrid and Cale stuff he hasn't let go of. And that all just sort of... picks away at your mental threads until all you can do is... well... curl up in a corner and... wallow."

"So I just... go in there and tell him to shut up?"

"Basically? It sounds... wrong, I know. But... The more you explain, the more I think you've been doing everything right."

May scoffed.

May had most certainly *not* done everything right. They just established that she was a fucking hormonal time bomb.

"Why can't Astrid or Cale do this?"

"We tried. A *lot*," Astrid said. "He... just shrugs us off."

How the fuck could Naina say May did everything right? *May* did this to Leo. And now *she* was the one they were saying needed to help?

"I mean... If this all centers around Cale and Astrid, he probably *wouldn't* listen to them, not unless he decided to take an idea for his own. But... it sounds like he listens to you, May? I just... if..." Naina's hand began to fidget with her shirt. "If his drinking problem is as bad as you're saying... Um... That's... That's... well..."

May leaned forward, attempting to meet Naina's eyes. "That's what?"

Naina didn't immediately respond, leaving her eyes averted.

"Naina," Astrid said. May looked over and also found Astrid staring intently at the redhead.

Naina's mouth opened a few moments before she spoke, "I mean... Severe and untreated rejection sensitivity has a very high correlation to... um... self-harm. If Leo is... drinking like you said he is... That's... not something you recover from by yourself. He *needs* support. Professional."

The ground fell out beneath May. Her entire body felt like it evaporated into nothingness at the image of Leo... continuing to spiral deeper.

A helicopter passed overhead, shaking the ground beneath them, but May barely noticed.

May felt the gaze of both Naina and Astrid fall on her. Her heart was still wriggling on the floor, gasping for breath.

"Just so I'm fucking clear," May said. She slowly stood to her feet, then took a few steps away, staring off at the distant cabins.

"We just established that I'm a fucking ticking time bomb of hormones," May said.

"Um... It's a bit reductionist, but, yes?"

"And we established that Leo can talk circles around me logically; I have no fucking chance to fight him rationally," May said.

"Uh... Well... Intelligence and processing style are... um... but in this case... He's had *years* to branch the logical paths on this. So... Yes. Probably. He will be able to rationalize endlessly; you can't stop him," Naina said.

"And we established that Leo is on some path of self-destruction that only *I* can stop?"

"Well, or one of you calls 988. It's a valid path," Naina said.

May opened her mouth to fight the assertion. The image of Leo having professionals show up at his door felt... wrong.

But... if she failed... well...

May tucked that option away in the back of her head.

"And it was laid out *quite* plainly that I am simply *not* equipped to help Leo based on a difference in... timing window preferences."

"Um. No, that's not what I said." Footsteps sounded from behind as Naina stepped up next to May. "Cognitive processing is prone to spiraling and rabbit-holing. You have a superpower he doesn't... Intuitive isn't... intuitive for him. You have... um... emotional and holistic intuition. Um... gut instinct, street smarts, while he relies on expertise, basically."

"What, the ability to just be 'mostly right'? You're fucking saying my ability to think less is a fucking 'super power'?" May cocked an eye over.

"There's no such thing as 'perfectly right', not within our capabilities to observe and process, at least. Once you accept that... It's just about *how much* thought you give it. So... depending on the context... yes?"

May crossed her arms and squeezed her eyes shut. Her head was throbbing.

She was saying May *should* just go with whatever popped in her head?

No, that wasn't right.

She was saying she needed to balance it. May needed to slow down, and Leo needed to speed up.

"May, he's going to make the game, the battlefield, about logic. Just don't let him. You've always been good at this," Astrid said. May heard the rustling of clothes and flat heels on gravel as Astrid joined the two of them in staring out at the cottages.

May took a deep breath.

She was his tank.

And she loved him.

May looked to either side, finding Naina and Astrid standing with clothes rippling slightly in a passing breeze.

"You really trust me with this?"

"We have our fallback plan," Astrid said. Naina nodded. "But... yes. You've always been good for him."

"How the fuck can you say that? I left him. I broke his heart."

"Um... *why* did you leave?" Naina asked.

"What?" May glanced over. "Does it matter?"

"Yes," Astrid said. Naina nodded again.

May let the scene play through her mind, the feelings she would process, the contradictory thoughts.

"I think I always knew something was wrong in my highs. I know how I hurt people. I know how my opinion of people will just... flip on a fucking dime. I know how-"

"What?" Naina asked, glancing over. May could practically feel her scanning her.

May glanced back at Astrid, who seemed just as baffled as May at Naina's confusion.

"I... sometimes I hear something about someone and it just... I fucking... I *feel* my opinion on them just... change. I've... done it with Cale a few times, too. That's... Bipolar, right?"

Naina's mouth fell open, and her eyes widened in some sort of recognition. She shook her head.

"Um. OK. OK. Is that... why you left Leo last time?" Naina asked, and she began pacing in a tight circle, her hands shaking at her sides.

May nodded. "Every time." The way Naina was looking at her made her mouth run dry.

"Oh... OK... Um... OK..." Naina looked at the ground while fidgeting with her fingers. "That's... I mean... Oh, I didn't prepare for this... If that happens, leaving is the right choice. Don't... don't act on whatever you think, not immediately. That's... that's how you hurt people. In Leo's case, it's... going to make him worse. If you recognized that was happening and Gamfest, and that's why you left, you did the right thing. Splitting is... um... Please. *Please* find a licensed professional."

Splitting?

"I... did the right thing?" May asked cautiously.

"I mean... It might not feel like it. Doing the right thing doesn't always have ideal results. Even with bipolar, professional recommendation when you find yourself in a hypomanic episode without medication is to just... remove yourself from situations that can cause spontaneous harm. If the alternative to leaving was staying and saying worse things to Leo... yes. Any time you left because you knew nothing good was going to follow... Yes. You were doing the best with what you knew at the time. Not... not many people can do that in the heat of the moment, with hormones flowing."

A soft breeze rippled between the three of them.

Then the world flipped.

May continued to watch Naina pace, but nothing around her registered.

She swallowed.

In a single moment, her mind ran through every single time she stormed off on Leo. Every time she distanced herself out of fear of what she would do or say.

She had been protecting him from a piece of her she didn't understand.

The thought provided her just the slightest semblance of fulfillment that *might* be describable as "pride".

How much of this whole situation had been the two of them doing the right thing, but for reasons they didn't understand?

"I did the right thing..." May muttered.

She just... hadn't been equipped to do better. She had always been doing the best she could.

"Um. Yes. You did," Naina said.

"I still hurt him," May said.

"Yes. Yes, you did," Naina acknowledged.

May's vision widened enough to find Astrid. Astrid nodded in agreement. She looked back to Naina.

"What you just did..."

"Therapy," Naina said. "Some cases just finding the right environment--the right workplace, the right relationship-- is enough. Others... you need an uninvolved, educated, third party. But... yeah. Therapy... and... well... pride. Never forget the pride."

Pride...

A huff of air escaped May's nose, not quite a laugh, at the thought.

That's what they were, right? The Pride?

May swallowed again, looking over to Astrid, who smiled and nodded in encouragement.

May was definitely not there yet.

But...

"Didn't you *hate* therapy, Astrid?" May asked, thinking back to her conversation with Leo.

Astrid sighed, "I... didn't think I needed it. I approached it in bad faith. And the professional I had... I don't think we were a good fit. You can't just... show up and expect results."

May nodded to Naina, "Then why can't we get Red to-"

Naina shook her head vigorously. "Good faith requires legally protected and enforced confidentiality to avoid any confessions coming with added pressures that exacerbate rather than alleviate. I... even talking to you right now... Navigating the boundaries on this discussion with a friend is... um... hard, and... risky."

"May, you don't need to fix him. You just need to get him to acknowledge he has a problem," Astrid offered.

"You *shouldn't* try to fix him," Naina said. "You're not equipped. He just needs to know he's worth the effort."

Mother *fucker*.

She could do that.

"Give me the ice packs. Let's end this fucking cycle," May said.
