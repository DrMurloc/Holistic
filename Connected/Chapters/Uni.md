**Uni**

**<Exhales a cosmic breath that had been held since before the conception of reality.>**

**We did it.**

**No.** ***They*** **did it.**

***All*** **of them.**

"Are you kidding me? Why is she so short? And what is that pout! I don't pout!" Astrid exclaimed as the movie started.

"You're really going to talk through this, aren't you. I see we're getting the added commentary with this viewing," Cale noted.

"Cale, I have *earned* the right!"

"Also, you used to pout," Cale observed.

"That's besides the point." Astrid turned her head and gave the most potent pout the world had ever seen.

"Is it, though?"

The exchange earned chuckles through the scattered guests as the on-screen couple mirrored the bickering with arguments of hot dogs and tacos.

**If I were to be quite honest, I was becoming increasingly concerned about the possibility of this search requiring transfinite time. The multiverse equivalent of a binary search only goes so far when dealing with countably infinite branches off of countably infinite branches.**

**When I started, I likely should have run the math on the probability... At this point, I wouldn't be able to compute it.**

**Not in my current state.**

Astrid exclaimed, "I *never* said 'It's not like I like you or anything'. Who wrote this script?"

"Technically, you did," Cale noted.

"That was *our* moment. And it wasn't like this... this... *anime trope*..." Astrid huffed.

"Teeeeechnically..." Cale laughed.

"You're an idiot." Astrid punched him in the shoulder, at the same exact time that the on-screen Astrid did. It earned a new round of laughter from the room.

"Astrid! Are you armored up over your own movie?" Cale wrapped an arm over Astrid's shoulders.

"It's not like I like it, or anything, baka." Astrid gave another pronounced pout, crossed her arms, then kissed her fiancé.

**Regardless.**

**Here we are. Ideally, the Lucas and Cass in this timeline also turn out OK... but that's a thought for much later. They'll be fine as long as no one tries to separate them.**

The door to the room opened, and all heads turned.

There stood, as if presenting themselves, the last missing pieces. Hands held, with warm smiles that left the room feeling complete.

"Yeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeees!" A scream erupted from the energetic Zoey, who dashed across the room and flung her arms around May.

"Why is MotherFucker here?" Sho asked in slightly forced English. All heads turned to him with an air of shock at the only English words anyone in the room had heard escape his lips.

"That's her game tag! To clarify!" Leo announced to the room. It did not, in fact, clarify much to anyone outside the core ten.

May and Leo eventually stepped over to the reserved empty seats next to Cale and Astrid.

**I just really needed to have trust in Naina. I knew I had picked her for a reason.**

May took her seat.

Leo nodded Cale to the side, and they found a secluded corner.

Neither made eye contact.

Leo started, "I'm sorry. I fucked up so bad."

"It's behind us, Leo."

"No, it's not."

**Now, Cale knew Leo's thought and speech patterns. The three of them, Astrid included,** ***had*** **forged each other, after all. A decade of pattern recognition leaves you able to hear every single word unsaid, breaks in the expected rhythm. It's almost... telepathic.**

**So Cale heard** ***everything*** **in those three words.**

Cale stepped forward, and Leo found himself embraced for the second time that day.

**Leo knew Cale's thought and speech patterns. A decade of pattern recognition leaves you able to hear every single word unsaid, breaks in the expected rhythm.**

**So Leo understood the novel implications that came with the first hug the two had ever exchanged. He understood the implications of a forgiveness and apology coming from a man who had built his life on never looking back.**

**He didn't need Cale to say it.**

**Which made it mean so much more when Cale did anyway.**

"You did the right thing."

To most outsiders watching across the room, watching best friends shaking with silent sobs and wet faces, the assumption would be that it was over one of the two entering a committed life of happiness.

But as eyes turned back to the two, Astrid knew. May knew. Zoey and Naina knew.

It was two best friends leaving a committed life of self-hatred.
