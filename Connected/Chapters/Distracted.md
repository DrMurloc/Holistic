## Distracted

**Zoey**

Zoey was unraveling.

She felt... emotions. Not Zoey-emotions, but *not-Zoey* emotions.

She didn't like the not-Zoey emotions.

Naina’s hoodie enveloped her like a cloud. The hood was pulled up over Zoey’s frayed and uncombed hair, and she clutched her arms over her stomach as if hugging the redhead herself. Each breath she took wafted in with the faint fragrance of Naina’s unscented shampoo.

"Zoe? Zoe!"

Zoey shook her head and turned to the speaker. Kev. Right, she was with friends.

Not-Naina friends.

"I'm sorry. What did I miss?" Zoey returned, and she stitched on her smile, taking in her surroundings.

When did they get to the dorm lounge? Weren't they just in the library?

Zoey sat on the couch, her hand lightly resting on the arm, where Naina's head was not. The others, Kev, Mia, and Justin, stood a few steps away, staring.

"Cafe, bubble tea. You game?" Kev stated.

"Yes! Tapioca would be the perfect distraction." Zoey stood up assertively.

"I don't think you need any more distraction, cuz." K ev smirked.

He turned with the others and began to walk. Zoey lingered a moment, staring back at the couch. The couch was surely also feeling the impact of her absence, poor thing.

Zoey walked briskly to catch up to the others.

Walking? That wasn't right.

She tried to skip, but couldn't quite find the rhythm.

Five days. Zoey had gone five days without seeing Naina. Was Zoey so hopeless that she shut down after only five days?

Absolutely, she was.

Naina said she had a schedule change to which she was acclimating. Zoey knew that changes in routine hit her hard, but five days? Of course, they still texted, but it wasn't quite the same.

"Zoey!" Mia's voice came from behind, and Zoey turned and tilted her head.

She had turned down a branch through the quad, towards the lake. Mia and the others stood on the other branch. Towards bubble tea. Zoey looked back over her shoulder, where Not-Mia’s messy bun bounced annoyingly. People were *not* allowed to have the same hair cut.

Zoey stapled her smile back on; the damn thing kept falling off, and she ran back to her friends.

"Girl, where is your head?" Kev said.

Mia leaned in. "Are you OK? You're like... extra 'Zoey' today."

"Yeah! I'm great, why?" Zoey failed miserably at a grin. They were so wrong. This was not-Zoey.

"Where's Naina? Did you two break up?" Mia asked.

"What! No! We weren't... " A dozen words attempted to escape Zoey's mouth at once. "I don't know where she is. I think she's... busy..."

Zoey trailed off as her eyes caught a flash of red hair.

"Anyways, so the car was coming, right-" Kev started.

The haircut was wrong. Maybe Naina cut it shorter? But the green hoodie... And the height was right.

Zoey darted forward, tackling her friend from behind with a hug and a series of completely uncontained giggles.

"Oh! I'm sorry!"

Wait.

That wasn't Naina's voice.

Zoey backed up a few steps, studying this new not-Naina who dared to have green eyes and freckles.

Zoey pursed her lips, slightly frustrated. Yet another not-Zoey emotion. Was this how Astrid felt all the time? It was terrible.

"Who even are you?" Zoey demanded.

"I'm... Mark?" he said with a weak smile.

**Eeeeeeey, there he is!**

It was a cute smile. But not "Naina cute".

Zoey looked down at her clenched hands. This boy didn't do anything wrong. This was a Zoey problem, not a Mark problem.

Zoey flashed the widest grin she could muster, managing to get out, "Your hair's nice."

She spun on her heel and walked away, leaving the not-Naina baffled.

Zoey was pretty sure her friends shouldn't have their mouths open that wide on a humid day in Pennsylvania, unless they were trying to collect gnats.

"Zoey?" Mia asked cautiously.

"What?" Zoey walked by them towards the cafe. Mia grabbed her shoulder and turned her around in the correct direction.

Kev and Justin burst out laughing as they approached the building.

Let them laugh; they don't understand *love*. They only understood *broom closets.*

The doors to the cafe opened, and Zoey's heart practically leapt into her throat.

There stood, behind the register, a certain adorable redhead.

Most assuredly *not* a not-Naina.

With no shortage of enthusiasm, she pushed through her friends and rushed to the counter, greeted by the smells of coffee and brown sugar.

"Oh! Zoey! I uh... Hi!" Naina smiled widely, looking slightly past Zoey. Zoey had really missed these heart flutters.

Zoey leaned against the counter in a poor attempt at nonchalance. This was how Cale did it right? The raised eyebrow, a smirk. Her leg was bouncing. He sometimes did that sometimes too, she guessed. She ignored the stares from several students sitting at the scattered wooden tables throughout the cafe.

“So when you said… ‘schedule change’?” Zoey said. She took in the apron. Cheap poly-blend. Blagh. *Not* sensory-friendly.

“Uh. Yes. My schedule changed.”

Zoey narrowed her eyes.

Why did Naina say it that way? Naina always spoke flatly. But that was a very flat flatly. Like she was *trying* to speak flatly.

Like she was *lying.*

Zoey’s eyes narrowed even further.

“Is that my hoodie?” Naina asked.

A chill ran down Zoey’s spine and her mouth opened to come up with some excuse other than “I swiped it the last time I was in your room”.

“It’s cute on you,” Naina said.

The chill melted into a puddle, and Zoey sighed with a smile.

Naina returned the smile.

Zoey’s legs wavered as her entire body threatened to dissolve into a mushy cream paste.

“Zoe?” Kev’s voice.

Oh crud bisquits. There was no way they would let *this* one go.

Zoey straightened up and plastered on her most serious face.

"Brown sugar tapioca with iced coffee, please," Zoey said as calmly as she could, emphasizing each word as if that made her sound more in control. Energy threatened to burst her at the seams, though; she was at risk of vibrating through the floor.

Kev snickered, and Zoey heard a soft thud as Justin smacked him in the shoulder. Those two needed to go to an amusement park.

The rest of the group put in their orders, and everyone paid, taking a seat at a large table in the far corner. Zoey's eyes lingered on Naina, who was still looking away.

"So, what in the hell was that?" Kev leaned in to study Zoey.

"What?" She asked, receiving a chorus of blank stares.

"Zoey." Mia rolled her eyes. "You two are clearly dating."

"Who, Naina? Never met her."

Turd muffins.

Justin tried this time. "And *Naina* is?"

"Her." Zoey nodded over her shoulder with a smile as if it explained everything.

"Zoey and her have been inseparable," Mia noted.

"We're separable! Look at us being separate right now!" Zoey exclaimed, gesturing to the space that divided her from her red-headed obsession.

The table exchanged glances.

Zoey could keep this up for days. She was a master of deflection.

A mistress of deflection?

Zoey contemplated for a moment, then determined that she was a deflection aficionado. One of Naina's fanfics used that word; it was a cool word.

Zoey took the moment to steal a glance back at her adorable redhead.

Naina was very focused on placing the bubble tea tops *just right,* and Zoey’s heart oozed.

Zoey wasn't pining.

Naina was just such a *good* friend, like the best friend.

The only friend Zoey ever needed.

A friend with such kissable lips and the softest hair that fit *just* perfectly between Zoey’s fingers and Zoey wanted to latch onto her like a koala and bury her face into the soft curve of her neck and bathe in the essence of her post-ramen spicy chashu scent and turn into her weighted blanket while she read silly fanfics about whatever “knotting” meant.

OK, maybe Zoey was pining.

Zoey screamed internally. Why did this need to be so complicated? Complicated things were dumb. Love *wasn't* complicated. People made it complicated. Was she making it complicated?

She turned her focus back to her friends.

Kev was mid-story. "--he wasn't even looking!"

"What? Like he just drove through and hit the kid? Are they OK?"

"No one knows, cuz."

She turned away from the boring conversation, watching Naina count straws and contemplate how to carry four cups. Zoey tingled, eliciting a bout of squirms. She wanted to go help so badly.

A chair pulled up next to her.

Mia leaned in and whispered, "That bad?"

"What? No!" Zoey turned sharply.

"Zoey, it doesn't take a literature major to identify reciprocal longing."

"Naina calls it mutual pining," Zoey said.

Mia groaned, "She's into that fanfic trash too."

Zoey decided this wouldn't be a good time to mention how often she had caught Mia reading Zoey's phone over her shoulder.

Zoey growled. "Fine. She makes my insides want to pop."

"You need an inciting incident, Zoey. Tell her," Mia said.

"I don't know. I think we're already very 'incited', and I don't want any more incidents. It's not that simple." Zoey sighed.

Mia held up a finger as Naina approached the table with two drinks and handed them to the boys. A short moment later, she returned with theirs.

Zoey turned to accept hers, and their fingers overlapped during the cup handoff.

*There! Her hand! It lingered! It lingered!*

Or did it?

Was Zoey just looking for excuses? She always blew these things out of proportion.

Naina smiled and walked back to the register.

"Zoe, oh my god, your face. *That* is longing. She has to know." Mia held her hand up to her mouth with a delicate chuckle.

"No! She's... bad at social cues. It makes it very complicated. If I'm wrong and she rejects me, or *worse*, I make her uncomfortable... I don't want to do that to her. We're happy now."

Zoey wilted with an uncharacteristic whimper.

They *were* happy, right? Of course they were. This is what happy looks like.

How could Zoey even explain?

If Mia knew Zoey, the *real* Zoey, she would understand.

Zoey was just an attention-deficit mess who had been subject to a series of verbal and physical positive punishment as retribution for her tendency to “speak out of line” due to a combination of cognitive-forward non-linear processing styles and a low working memory that encouraged her to speak before she could intuitively process the shape of the words. The self-iterative loop of both hyperprocessing while inhibiting said hyperprocessing as taboo with the occasional surge of intrusive thoughts left her in a self-hating loop of de-masking and re-masking without any capability of understanding her own core identity.

She was also probably dylexic. Dylactic? Dactylic? No, that was poetry.

**You’re looking for dyslexic, Zoey.**

Dyslexic, yeah, that one. Thanks, random voice in my head.

**Wait, what?**

*That* girl, *that* Zoey wasn't worth a Naina. That girl wasn't even worth a not-Naina. Naina didn't seem to care about that, though, which made Naina perfect. But it left Zoey... and not-Zoey... with complicated thoughts.

**Did she hear me?**

And complicated things were dumb.

Zoey consulted the wisdom in her drink.

It told her to just talk to Naina and see if she wanted to return to their routine.

It was truly an experienced and knowledgeable iced coffee. Must be the tapioca.

At that moment, the door opened, and a somewhat familiar face walked in.

"Naina's like Asher, but different," Zoey noted, nodding to the quiet boy.

Mia looked over her shoulder only briefly, before turning her chair away and burying her face in the fourth book Zoey had seen her with that week.

The boy placed his order at the counter, then paused.

"Has Asher met Naina?" Miles asked from several seats over, causing both Zoey and Mia to jolt.

He already had a drink?

When did he sit down? Zoey didn't even hear him come in. She swore he could teleport with special... Chinese ninjutsu or something.

"Miles, stop doing that," Mia said with a hand to her chest.

“Nobody expects the Miles inquisition,” Miles stated in his deadpan voice.

Mia and Zoey stared blankly.

“Like the Spanish Inquisition?”

“Why would you expect the Spanish Inquisition?” Zoey asked.

Miles sighed. “It’s Monty Py- how do you not know- you know what, whatever.” He nodded to the counter where Naina leaned back and forth in an attempt to figure out what part of her Asher’s gaze was tracking while he studied her. "But Naina. She hasn't received the Asher special?"

"No." The back of Zoey's mind thrummed with anticipation for what Asher would whip up for Naina.

She had time, though She needed an icebreaker. For Naina. She needed Naina back. And she needed to be smooth about it.

She needed something somewhere between "Yo" and "My entire life is collapsing without you, please don't leave me, I love you."

The boy lingered at the counter after paying, leaning forward towards Naina.

Naina froze in place, as Asher studied her from several angles. Zoey knew what it was like to get catalogued by the boy; it was pretty unnerving when you didn't expect it.

Maybe "Hey, girl, want to come back to my place?"... No, not quite right.

Naina looked in Zoey's direction, likely wondering if this boy was about to get tackled.

Zoey made no such move.

Asher was harmless.

He was already mumbling with notebook in hand as he walked towards a seat near them.

Maybe Zoey could channel Naina. How about, "I have borderline destructive underlying anxiety catalyzed by a deeply rooted fear of abandonment stemming back to critical and unhealthy relationships in my youth, exacerbated by an intolerance to minor changes in daily rhythm. Please come back."

Close. Not quite, though.

Zoey grabbed Asher's arm and directed him into the seat next to Mia.

Mia, to nobody’s surprise, stood up and moved to another table.

Eh, it was worth a try.

"OK, Asher, this needs to be a good one," Zoey stated, distracted from her distracted state.

"Zoey, they're all good ones," Miles countered.

Asher ignored them, flipping through his notebook and mumbling a list of adjectives under his breath.

"But this is *Naina*; it has to be the best."

"Red and green... Fire and life? No... more timid... Sky... a sunset... and a forest...." Asher mumbled to himself in that low and gravelly voice that seemed to just reverberate straight to the core.

Both Zoey and Miles leaned forward, listening to the rapid fire of adjectives and phrases.

"Verdant..." He paused, then gave a grunt of approval at himself, and his voice grew too quiet to hear.

"I like verdant," Miles noted. Zoey nodded, her legs bounced under the table.

Naina managed to pick the perfect time to drop off Asher's drink.

Asher flipped a page in his notebook and went still. The sudden quiet made Miles and Zoey tilt their heads, instinctively waiting. Naina hesitated, her hand lingering on the taro milk tea she'd just placed down.

Asher's lips began to move, his voice loud enough to carry, but still dripping with gravelly seductive charm: "Verdant meadows of glistening emerald, embraced in the tender afterglow of sunset."

Naina froze yet again, rather adorably, Zoey noted.

Zoey spent several moments bottling up energy like a pressurized cannon, then emitted a rather undignified, but *very* Zoey, squeal.

"Thank... thank you?" Naina stated, her eyes darting between Asher and Zoey.

Asher grunted in acknowledgment, jotting down several notes. His mumbling had ended, and the focus shifted to analysis and documentation.

"Naina, this is Asher. We like Asher," Zoey noted, hands and legs still vibrating with ripples of release.

"Speak for yourself," Mia mumbled. Zoey noted the girl hiding her face. "The prose was purple at best. He just strung a bunch of words together with no meaningful subtext or nuance."

Zoey was working on those two. They were too good together.

"Was... was that flirting?" Naina asked.

"No, strangely. It's just Asher's equivalent of a napkin doodle for practice. He likes the way the words sound out loud." Zoey noted.

The boy didn't even look up as they talked about him. He only ever really talked in one-on-one situations; group settings were complicated for him.

"Oh! External processing and verbal self-stimulation! That's really cool, actually." Now it was Naina who was studying and cataloging Asher, with a faint smile.

Miles and Zoey exchanged glances, and Zoey grinned with pride for her redhead.

Miles kicked Zoey under the table. Zoey glared and kicked him back. Miles shook his head and mouthed “Tell her.”

Oh, right, there was something Zoey needed to do, by orders of tapioca.

"Naina, I miss you. Come hang out!" Zoey exclaimed.

"Oh, yeah. Sorry, I've been distracted with preparing for... holiday expenses. My shift is over in two hours, though, I'll come by." Naina smiled.

That smile, set beneath two verdant meadows of glistening emerald, embraced in the tender afterglow of sunset, seemed to unwind the world, and chaos unfurled into order.

What was left of Zoey's anxiety was sucked out as if by a giant bubble tea straw.

The power and insight of tapioca were never to be underestimated.
