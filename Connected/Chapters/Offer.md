He found none in either Spanish or Japanese that were powerful enough.

So he stepped in and kissed her.

**Lara**

The renewed energy of the promotion coursed through her veins, invigorated by the feeling of Sho's lips.

They stood there, in the middle of the stone path, for what was likely a full minute. It likely would have been an eternity that she milked that raw energy, the *thrill* of having both a path and a partner.

Her phone buzzing in her pocket cut that eternity off quite early.

She contemplated ignoring it.

But it opened a thread in her brain that she just couldn't shut.

It was a knot.

Lara groaned and stepped back, pulling the phone up.

Mom.

She raised the phone and nearly hit the answer button, but paused. There was a different knot she needed to untangle quickly.

She took a deep breath and organized her thoughts, searching for simple words.

For some odd reason, she could picture how she would type it, but not how she would say it. She also wanted Sho to soak in the words, not just hear them.

Lara reached over and slid Sho's phone out of his pocket; he did nothing to stop her.

After a few moments, she typed out in his translator app, *"Even if you think turning down the job was the wrong decision, I think you made the right one. I know you say I'm the smart one and have all the answers. If that is the case, then what does it mean that I think you are the smart one? You always think. You wouldn't make decisions based on nothing. I trust you."*

She studied his face as he read for a moment, watching as his eyes darted across the phone, then tapped the answer button on her own phone and hopped back up onto the raised stone embankment, shifting focus to whatever nonsense her Mom had for her.

**Sho**

Sho stared at the message.

She... trusted him.

His stare remained on the phone as he walked over to the wall opposite where Lara walked and sank to a sitting position, tilting his head back against the stone.

His eyes closed.

Lara spoke faster than he could keep up with. He could pick a few words here or there, words like "move", "el país", "la isla".

The smartest girl in the world trusted *him*. And said *he* was the smart one?

But she didn't know. She didn't know what Sho had done. All the things he *hadn't* done.

If Sho was so smart, then why did he-

The phone dug into his fingers as he squeezed, embracing the message from Lara and pushing the thought out. He did not want to spur the awakening of the strongest demon.

Lara trusted him.

Maybe that was all he needed.

Sho smiled.

"I'm going home," Lara said.

Sho stopped smiling.

His eyes snapped open as the warmth drained to the gravelly ground beneath him. Sho was standing over him, phone in her pocket.

"Wait. Sorry. I need to stop leading with that in a month. I'm going to go home, for... Probably a few months." Lara paused as Sho blinked, processing the sentence. She fired off into her fast-talk, "Mom is moving back to la isla in febrero. I think it's a really good decision; her family is there, and she likes the culture much more, and she de verdad dislikes the USA, and I think it would get her away from her problems with Dad, and-"

Lara stopped and took a deep breath.

The way she licked her lips occasionally, the way her eyes darted, Sho knew that it was anxiety.

She was worried about him.

**Lara**

She didn't have the right words.

Her mind raced, attempting to piece together an explanation.

The context it would take to explain to Sho just how impactful this would be for both Lara *and* her Mom was too much. And her Mom hadn't asked her to go home this time... just said she was going to move and that it would be difficult.

And somehow her not *asking* made Lara *want* to help? Was she being manipulative still? Just learning a new route to control Lara, or was she legitimately just treating Lara more like an equal?

Lara squeezed her eyes shut.

"I don't know how to explain. Mom is... Mom. But I-"

Any further words or spiraling were blocked by Sho's lips.

After a moment, he stepped back and held up his phone. Her message from before still sat on it, but trimmed. *"I know you say I'm the smart one and have all the answers. If that is the case, then what does it mean that I think you are the smart one? You always think. You wouldn't make decisions based on nothing. I trust you."*

Sho trusted her...

She should stay.

Sho slid his phone into his pocket and took her hands by the fingers just as she had done earlier.

"Your Mom is your biggest 'messy'," he said.

"But I spent *years* trying to fix it... I needed to let it go. It would be wrong to try again, right?" Lara's gaze fell to the wall behind Sho, and she felt the anxiety pour out of her mouth. "I would be an idiot to think-"

"But you are not an idiot. You are considering it for a reason." Sho cut off her spiraling again.

Lara blinked.

She would be a hypocrite to ignore his point.

Lara shifted gears in her mind and let her thoughts stream. She began to pace again.

"Ever since that day a month ago, it's like she started trying? She talks about Dad less and doesn't 'correct' me as much? She still tries to help, but it's like she doesn't... care if I take her advice? Which makes me want to take it more? Is it wrong that I listen to her more when I think she cares less? That doesn't feel right... It's like... She's less manipulative. I think that's it. Like she's trying to be supportive and not... controlling. That is important, right?"

Lara came to a stop, finding herself staring down the path at a distant couple posing and taking selfies. From above the wall, the sound of laughter from some passing kids echoed into the aqueduct.

Sho finally spoke, "Yes. That distinction is important."

She should go.

Lara found words spilling out of her mouth as she turned to Sho. "Long distance is hard. I've read all sorts of studies on-"

"I've read them too. We have a month," Sho said.

They had a month.

A month was forever.

A month was nothing.

"Ok," Lara said. She tilted her forehead against Sho's and repeated, "Ok."

**Sho**

A month.

A month was forever.

A month was nothing.

Sho's mouth had run dry, but he stepped up and kissed her one last time before they turned down the path in silence.

The end of their current season was a month away.

He had just told the greatest thing to ever happen to him, his magical female lead, to just... leave.

He could have said stay. She probably would have listened.

He never would have. He trusted her.

The sun had begun to dip, casting them in a shadow that carried the threat of evening twilight.

Demons lurked in those shadows. Memories of what it was like to be alone. They didn't dare encroach while Lara walked with him... his shield, his holy weapon of light... but... *three months* without her.

The anxiety, the spiraling, the darkness, the fog...

His parent's dissaproval.

He didn't want to go back.
