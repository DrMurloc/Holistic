Blaire sighed as the last of the Tuscan sun faded from view. This new wave of writers was going to see him out of a job.

Not because they were good, he clearly wasn't threatened on creative capability, but because they were so *bad*.

Everyone was so caught up in Alternate Universes with the same Time Traveler, or a dumb magic school. What happened to true art, true romance?! These kids needed *polin* to do their writing for them!

Granted the Wolfman and Sharkman high school fic was pretty good reads. But there wasn't anything he could work with. Where was his inspiration? His *muse*?

It had been six months since his last movie: “Will They or Won't They: 3” And he was desperate for something new. But the well was dried up. Creative writing was *dead*. He would only be left with a legacy of seventy two romances, one fictional biography, and an action film he refused to acknowledge.

Truly a travesty. He took a bite from his cheese plate and sipped his twenty year Montepulciano Nobile. The soft rolling Tuscan hills extended out from the small castle town he was staying in. Low murmurs of activities came from various restaurants and bars below him.

He took a deep breath. Maybe this was what he would need to settle on. A life of simple travel, a lost soul with no guidance. Forced to settle for the most modest of five star hotels, only one massage a week. Who knew… maybe he would have to buy *non Italian shoes.*

He perished the thought. No, there had to he something of quality out there! He just had to dig deeper. He changed the filter to be most recent.

He skimmed for a moment, confused. Who the the hell was Astrid and Cale? Fourty new fics? *I swear I'm going to lose it if these are another two characters from that demon hunting show.*

He searched for the source material. A blog? What was this…

He dug in, losing himself in the story. The more he read the more he felt it piece together. Yes… yes… yes!

This was perfect! Not the fanfic of course, it was obviously derivative self insert trash, but this blog! He must have it, *needed* to have it.

A girl and a boy… who fight… but they secretly love each other? Who could have ever thought of that perfection! It was almost too real, maybe it would distance fans… but no, this movie needed to be made.

They didn't know it yet, but Cale and Astrid would be a household name by the time he was done with them!
